Laboratory No. 2: Least Squares

Course: Elements of Numerical Calculus 

Institution: Department of Mathematics, Faculty of Exact and Natural Sciences, University of Buenos Aires.

Laboratory Description

The main objective of this laboratory is to solve polynomial least squares fitting problems, analyzing numerical implementation through different methods, the phenomenon of overfitting, the conditioning of the matrices involved, and the stability comparison between normal equations, QR factorization, and singular value decomposition (SVD).

The program implements and analyzes the following numerical aspects:
Data Generation: Creation of simulated experimental points using a sine function with Gaussian noise.
Resolution Methods: Implementation and comparison of three different approaches to solve the least squares problem: normal equations,QR factorization, and SVD.
Overfitting: Visual and graphical evaluation of the model's behavior as the polynomial degree increases.
Conditioning and Stability: Analysis of the condition number of the matrices involved to determine the limits of numerical reliability for each method.
